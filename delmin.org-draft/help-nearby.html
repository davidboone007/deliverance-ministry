Monday nights in Oklahoma City

Free, one-on-one ministry with trained ministers, every Monday except major holidays. Whether you live nearby or are willing to travel, you are welcome.

Your evening

  1. 5:30 pm Introduction: Jesus' works today, how evil spirits gain access, your authority in Christ, and staying free.
  2. 6:30 pm Door-closing prayer together. required before personal ministry
  3. 7:30 pm Personal ministry with a small team. Sessions end by 10:30 pm at the latest.

Before you come

  • Print the Problem List and circle anything that applies to you.
  • Reserve your Monday with the form on this page.
  • Plan to stay the whole evening.
  • No childcare is available. See help for children.
