What happens in ministry

  1. Learn. What Jesus did and still does, how evil spirits gain a foothold, and the authority believers have in His name.
  2. Close the doors. Forgive, repent and renounce in a door-closing prayer, cancelling any legal right evil spirits have claimed.
  3. Break ungodly soul ties and receive inner healing for past wounds.
  4. Deliverance. Evil spirits are commanded to leave in Jesus' name.
  5. Healing. We pray for physical healing and for the fullness of the Holy Spirit.
  6. Stay free. You leave with simple practices to walk in freedom.
