About the ministry

This is the enduring ministry of Jesus Christ.

Jesus healed the sick and cast out demons, and He sent His followers to do the same: “Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, and cast out demons. Freely you received, so freely give.” Matthew 10:8 Deliverance Ministries exists to continue that work and to equip the body of Christ to carry it on.

Since 1984, teams of trained volunteer ministers in Oklahoma City have prayed with tens of thousands of people figure? for freedom, inner healing and physical healing. Ministry has always been free.

What we do

  • Free one-on-one ministry every Monday night in Oklahoma City
  • Free ministry by phone, Zoom or FaceTime, with a guide to prepare at home
  • Free training for believers who want to minister
  • Teaching and ministry for churches and groups

How we minister

We teach before we minister. People forgive, repent and renounce what opened the door to the enemy, and then, in Jesus' name, we command evil spirits to leave and pray for healing. We work as teams, so no one ministers alone and the work never depends on one person.
