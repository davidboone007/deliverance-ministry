Help from anywhere

Can't come on a Monday? Three simple steps at home, then a trained minister meets with you by phone, Zoom or FaceTime. It's free.

Pray the door-closing prayer required

Until you meet with a minister, this prayer is a good place to begin. It doesn't replace one-on-one deliverance, but it lets you start shutting doors the enemy may have used and stand on what Jesus has already done for you while you wait.

Pray it aloud as the video leads you, using the door-closing prayer handout. The Door-Closing Prayer at a glance outlines each part of the prayer on two pages, so you can see where you are and come back to it. En español

What would you like freedom from?

Check any area that is a problem for you now, or that has followed you through life. Tap more to see the details under each one and check those too. Check as many as apply. This list is kept confidential and helps your minister prepare.

Pray first: Father God, search me and know my heart; try me and know my thoughts. Holy Spirit, show me anything in me that is not of You. Help me to be honest and unafraid, and lead me in the everlasting way. In Jesus’ name, amen. Based on Psalm 139:23-24

✓

What happens next

A minister will reach out to you to schedule your deliverance session by phone, Zoom or FaceTime. Until then, keep praying the door-closing prayer, and rest in this: “Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest.” Matthew 11:28
